About the Artist

Enny Sonik is a visual artist whose primary medium is charcoal pencil. Through his drawings, he explores the intricate relationship between humanity and nature, examining themes of economic balance, social inequality, and resilience in the face of adversity. His work serves as a visual dialogue about our shared environment and the enduring strength of the human spirit.
