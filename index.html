ABOUT ME

Enny Sonik is a visual artist whose primary medium is charcoal pencil. Through my drawings, I explore the intricate relationship between humanity and nature, examining themes of economic balance, social inequality, and resilience in the face of adversity. My works serve as a visual dialogue about our shared environment and the enduring strength of the human spirit.

I was born in Lagos, Nigeria, in the year 2005. My legal name is Kojo Solomon Setonji Eniola, but I am professionally known as Enny Sonik. As a self-thought artist, the journey of my art began at the age of ten, and then, in 2024, I fully realized my talent and ventured into art.
